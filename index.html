🚀 Quick Start 
// Initialize SDK
const Bradicoin = require('bradicoin-sdk');

const client = new Bradicoin({
    apiKey: 'your_api_key',
    apiSecret: 'your_api_secret',
    network: 'mainnet'
});

// Create payment
const payment = await client.createPayment({
    amount: 100,
    toAddress: 'BRD-XXXX...',
    callbackUrl: 'https://yoursite.com/webhook'
});

// Check balance
const balance = await client.getBalance('BrYourAddress');
console.log(balance);

A message from the Bradicoin team to you

First of all, thank you for being here. The simple fact that you opened this page already means a lot to us.

Bradicoin was born from a simple but powerful idea: to build a blockchain that truly belongs to everyone — free, transparent, decentralized, and open to any person, in any country, regardless of who they are or how much they have. We are not a company chasing profit. We are a community, a team of builders, dreamers, and believers who dedicate their time, energy, and hearts to keep this project alive and growing every single day.

This page exists for one reason only: to give those who truly want to support us — from their own free will — a way to do so. We are not, and we never will be, asking for forced donations of any amount. You are not obligated to contribute. You are not pressured. You are not being manipulated. There is no expectation, no requirement, no condition. The Bradicoin project will continue to exist, to evolve, and to remain completely free and accessible to everyone — whether you contribute or not. That is our promise.

We want to be absolutely transparent with you: Bradicoin is NOT a Ponzi scheme, and it never will be. We do not promise guaranteed returns. We do not pay old investors with new investors' money. We do not rely on recruitment. We do not hide behind fake numbers, fake promises, or fake profits. What we offer is a real, open-source, decentralized blockchain built on transparency, security, and community. Any investment you choose to make is a voluntary gesture of support — not a financial product, not a guaranteed profit, not a trap.

If you decide to invest in Bradicoin voluntarily, know that every single value is welcome — there is no minimum, no maximum, no "ideal" amount. Whether it is $1 or $10,000, every contribution carries the same weight to us, because it is not the number that matters. What matters is the intention behind it: the desire to see this project grow, to help us cover infrastructure costs, to support development, security audits, servers, and everything that keeps Bradicoin alive and improving for everyone.

But here is the most important thing we want you to remember: you do not need to donate to be part of this. Bradicoin belongs to all of us. If you never contribute a single cent, you will still have full access to the blockchain, the explorer, the tools, the community, and everything we build. You will still be welcomed, respected, and valued. The project is for you, with or without a donation.

We know that trust is earned, not demanded. And we know that too many projects in this space have abused people's hope, their faith, and their money. We refuse to be one of them. We choose honesty over hype. We choose transparency over shortcuts. We choose to build slowly, correctly, and with integrity — even if it takes longer, even if it's harder.

If you feel in your heart that you want to support us, thank you — from the bottom of our hearts. Your gesture, no matter how small, is a vote of confidence in a better, freer, more decentralized future. And if you choose not to contribute, that is perfectly fine too. We are still grateful that you are here, reading this, being part of our journey.

Thank you for your time. Thank you for your trust. Thank you for believing, or for simply being curious. Together, whether donating or not, we are building something that matters. ⚡💖
